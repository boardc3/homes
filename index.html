    Elevations

    Four sides, straight on.

    Orthographic projections of the same geometry. Roof and canopy follow the raked left block and the sloped right block exactly.

    Plan

    Fifteen squares, one ring.

    The platform is 606 in. × 396 in. overall (50 ft 6 in. × 33 ft). Gray cells are the living area; the three dashed white cells are the patio.

    • Platform tube, 4 in. × 4 in.
    • Bridge tube across the 12 in. gap
    • Gray living floor, 12 cells of 93 in. × 93 in.
    • White patio, 3 cells
    • Red tower columns, 12
    • Teal frame posts and canopy uprights

    Drainage

    Two slopes, each doing one job.

    Left block: 1 ft fall, north to south

    The roof frame and canopy hold 11 ft above the deck at the patio edge and fall a continuous 12 in. toward the south end, so water runs away from the patio. It is one unbroken slope across the frame, the patio extension and the south joists.

    Right block: 4 ft fall, tower to east edge

    The 2 × 3 block roof starts 14 ft above the deck at the tower and slopes to 10 ft at the far east edge. It has no north–south fall; it already drains east.
