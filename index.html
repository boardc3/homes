Elevations

Four sides, straight on.

Orthographic projections of the frame. The left block's roof and canopy fall a continuous 12 in. from north to south, and the right block slopes from the tower to its east edge.

Plan

Fifteen squares, one ring.

The platform is 606 in. × 396 in. overall (50 ft 6 in. × 33 ft). Gray cells are the interior: the 24 ft × 16 ft living area in the wider left block, and two 16 ft × 12 ft bedrooms in the deeper right block. The three dashed white cells are the patio.

  • Platform tube, 4 in. × 4 in., 97 in. pitch
  • Bridge tube across the 12 in. gap
  • Gray living floor, 12 cells of 93 in. × 93 in.
  • White patio, 3 cells
  • Red tower columns, 12, 15 ft above the deck
  • Teal frame posts and canopy uprights
  • Bedroom posts moved into the partition at y = 147.5 in.
  • 54 legs, 10 in. tall, on 10 in. × 10 in. plates

Drainage

Two slopes, each with one job.

Left block: 12 in. fall, north to south

The roof frame and canopy start 11 ft above the deck at the north (patio) edge and fall a continuous 12 in. to the south end, so water runs away from the patio. One unbroken slope covers the frame, the patio extension and the south joists.

Right block: 4 ft fall, tower to east edge

The 2 × 3 block roof starts 14 ft above the deck at the tower and slopes to 10 ft at the far east edge. It has no north–south fall because it already drains east.

Numbers

What it adds up to.

Lengths are total tube run computed from the model geometry. Areas use the 93 in. gray cells (living area) and the 606 in. × 396 in. outer footprint including the border ring. Enclosure finishes (ipe, charred cedar, glass, Corten) are a design concept for the renderings.
