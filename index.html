Plan

Fifteen squares, one ring.

The platform is 606 in. × 396 in. overall (50 ft 6 in. × 33 ft). Gray cells are the interior: two 12 ft × 16 ft bedrooms on the left and the 16 ft × 24 ft living area on the right. The three dashed white cells are the patio.

  • Platform tube, 4 in. × 4 in., 97 in. pitch
  • Bridge tube across the 12 in. gap
  • Gray living floor, 12 cells of 93 in. × 93 in.
  • White patio, 3 cells
  • Red tower columns, 12, 15 ft above the deck
  • Teal frame posts and canopy uprights
  • Bedroom post moved into the partition at x = 147.5 in.
  • 54 legs, 10 in. tall, on 10 in. × 10 in. plates

Drainage

Two slopes, each with one job.

Left block: 12 in. fall, north to south

The roof frame and canopy start 11 ft above the deck at the north (patio) edge and fall a continuous 12 in. to the south end, so water runs away from the patio. One unbroken slope covers the frame, the patio extension and the south joists.

Right block: 4 ft fall, tower to east edge

The 2 × 3 block roof starts 14 ft above the deck at the tower and slopes to 10 ft at the far east edge. It has no north–south fall because it already drains east.
